DRONE PHOTOGRAPHY & VIDEO SERVICES

Need Drone Video Services for Your Business, Property or Next Campaign?

Elatre provides drone video services and aerial photography that help businesses show their properties, projects and locations clearly. We plan the shoot around the story, then bring aerial footage, ground-level filming and editing together into content you can use on your website, in sales presentations and across social media.

For example, a hotel needs to show more than its roof. A developer needs to show more than an empty plot. A business needs more than a beautiful clip that nobody knows where to use. Start with the result you want the viewer to understand. We will work backward from there.

START WITH A REASON TO WATCH

What Should a Business Drone Shoot Actually Achieve?

The strongest aerial shot answers something. Where is this property? How large is the facility? What surrounds the hotel? How has the site changed? Why should a customer care about this location?

That is the difference between collecting footage and building a useful business asset. A wide establishing shot can explain the setting. A closer approach can reveal access, scale or design. Ground footage can show the people, product and experience that make the place worth choosing.

Our commercial drone services start with those decisions. We agree on the audience, the message and the places where the final content will appear before planning camera movements. You receive a clear brief rather than a shoot day built around improvisation.

Already planning a larger campaign? We can connect the aerial work with video production, photography and the marketing assets your team needs to launch it.

PLACES, PROJECTS AND PEOPLE

Which Businesses Can Use Drone Photography and Video?

Different buyers need different footage. The shoot should follow the decision your audience is making, whether that is booking a room, viewing a property, selecting a supplier or understanding a construction project.

Real estate and commercial property

Show how a building sits within its surroundings, where visitors arrive and what the property offers beyond individual rooms. Drone photography services can support listing galleries, leasing presentations and development marketing. Combine aerial views with interiors and ground-level details so the finished story feels complete. Agree on the boundaries and features that matter before the shoot.

Hotels, resorts and destinations

A room photograph does not explain the experience outside the window. Aerial footage can connect a hotel with its grounds, pool, coastline or surrounding landscape. Ground filming can then bring the guest experience into focus. Plan the final film around the reasons someone would book, with versions suited to the property website and social campaigns.

Construction and development

Help stakeholders follow a project without relying on a handful of unrelated site photographs. Scheduled aerial visits can document visible progress from agreed viewpoints. A completed-project film can also support bids, investor updates and marketing. Visual documentation is a different scope from surveying or technical inspection, so the required outputs should be clear from the first conversation.

Manufacturing, logistics and facilities

Give buyers a sense of the operation before they arrive. An aerial sequence can show the site, access and physical scale, while interviews and ground footage explain the work inside. We can connect this with manufacturing video production to build a film for sales, recruitment or company presentations.

Restaurants, venues and retail locations

Help customers picture the place they are considering. The right exterior footage establishes the location and atmosphere, while ground filming captures food, service, interiors and people. A restaurant advertisement needs both context and appetite. Pair aerial scenes with food and beverage video production rather than asking one overhead shot to carry the entire campaign.

Corporate brands and campaigns

Use aerial footage to establish the setting for a company story, brand announcement or promotional film. It can introduce an office, campus, destination or event venue before the film moves into the people and ideas behind the business. Where appropriate, we combine it with corporate video production so the sequence supports a clear message.

WHAT YOUR TEAM TAKES AWAY

What Do You Receive From Elatre's Drone Video Services?

Your project can include a finished aerial film, edited photographs and versions for your website, social channels or sales team. Your proposal lists exactly what we will deliver. A business that needs listing photographs has a different scope from one commissioning a campaign film and recurring social content.

The film and photographs

  • Edited aerial video: a defined film or sequence with an agreed duration, structure and finish.
  • Aerial photographs: selected, edited still images covering the locations and views in the brief.

Versions for each channel

  • Ground and aerial edits: one connected film when interviews, interiors, product scenes or people are part of the shoot.
  • Channel versions: landscape, vertical or square edits, with framing planned for those formats.

Finishing and file delivery

FROM A LOCATION TO A CAMPAIGN

How Would a Restaurant or Hotel Campaign Come Together?

Consider a venue that wants more bookings. An aerial opening can establish the setting, but the reasons to visit usually appear closer to the ground. The entrance, the welcome, the dining space and the experience need their own attention.

Show the complete visit

For this kind of brief, we would discuss a connected sequence: an exterior view that explains the location, an arrival shot, interiors, people at work and the details that make the venue recognizable. A restaurant may need food preparation and table scenes. A hotel may need rooms, shared spaces and the setting around the property.

Create content for each use

The main film can tell the full story. Shorter edits can concentrate on a single reason to book, with photographs supporting the website and campaign. Each version needs its own purpose, even when the assets come from the same shoot.

Start with your venue

This is a planning example, not a fixed package. The final shot list depends on your venue, audience, access and budget. The useful question is what a customer needs to see before deciding to visit.

Why Bring the Shoot, the Edit and the Marketing Brief Together?

Aerial footage often becomes less useful when each supplier works from a different brief. The camera team captures one idea, the editor has another, and the marketing team later asks for a format nobody planned.

Elatre brings photography, video production, editors and marketing into the same conversation. That means the creative decisions can reflect the actual use of the content from the beginning. A website film can include space for a clear opening message. A paid campaign can plan for short versions. A sales team can request a film that answers the questions buyers repeatedly ask.

We have worked with more than 1,000 brands across our wider business. For your project, the useful conversation is specific: the examples relevant to your sector, the deliverables your team needs and the people responsible for completing them.

Do You Need Aerial Footage, an Edited Film or a Complete Shoot?

Choose the scope that solves the actual job. More footage is not automatically a better purchase.

Aerial capture

Best for: A team with its own editor that needs particular footage or photographs.

Agree before booking: Shot list, capture requirements, file handover and usage.

Edited aerial film

Best for: A property, site or venue that needs a finished video built mainly from aerial scenes.

Agree before booking: Film length, story, photography, music, revisions and delivery formats.

Combined business production

Best for: A brand that needs aerial footage, ground filming, people, interviews or product scenes in one campaign.

Agree before booking: Crew, locations, shoot schedule, main film and channel-specific edits.

Our recommendation follows the brief. If a straightforward aerial shoot covers the need, the quote should say so. If the story depends on interiors, staff or customer experience, plan that work before choosing a package.

Elatre's Five-Step Approach to a Business Drone Shoot

A useful process makes the creative work easier. Here is how we take the project from an initial requirement to footage your team can put to work.

1. Start with the viewer and the decision

Tell us what you are promoting and what someone should do after watching. We identify the main audience, the message and the channels. A leasing film, a hotel campaign and a construction update can involve the same location but require different shots. The brief gives everyone a shared reason for each scene.

2. Build the shot list around the location

We review the location, access, surroundings and production requirements before confirming the shoot plan. The aerial sequence should work with the rest of the content, not interrupt it. We also discuss practical constraints, weather arrangements and the ground footage needed to tell the story without depending on one type of shot.

3. Capture with the final edit in mind

On the shoot, the brief guides the footage. Establishing views, approach shots and details each have a role. When ground production is included, the crew plans transitions between the aerial setting and the people or activity below. This gives the edit a sequence to follow instead of a collection of unrelated highlights.

4. Edit for the message and the channel

Our editors shape the agreed footage into a film with a clear opening, useful pacing and a purposeful ending. For example, a website video may need more context than a short advertisement, while a vertical edit may need a different sequence. We prepare the versions listed in your proposal, then refine them through the agreed review process.

5. Deliver content ready for your team

We hand over clearly named files with guidance on how to use them. If Elatre also manages the campaign, our production and marketing teams coordinate when and where the content appears. If your team handles distribution, the files are organized so they can find the right version quickly. We can quote for additional formats or future updates when you need them.

What we need to quote

To give you a useful quote, we need the location, intended use, preferred timing and the outputs you expect. We then separate the shoot requirements from the editing and delivery scope so you can understand what you are buying.

What the quote covers

Travel, additional crew, special production requirements, raw footage, extra versions and revision rounds should be discussed before booking. We also explain how scheduling will work if weather or site conditions prevent the planned aerial shoot. Send your project requirements and we can recommend an appropriate scope.
